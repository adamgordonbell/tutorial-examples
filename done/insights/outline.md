Group by Module - Grouping your resources helps you gain insights into your infrastructure and sometimes allows you to learn things that may be different than you expected.

Group by Stack - For example, having so many storage resources may be a surprise so drilling in by module type or stack might help you gain the insights needed.

Group by Modified - Here you can see what's changing. This let's me focus in on what's curently happening and I modify this, and star it.
